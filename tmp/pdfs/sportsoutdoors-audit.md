## Source differences and qualifications

- Other Exercise & Fitness Accessories is present under Fitness Accessories & Sports Medicine. Jai Alai Sets is present under Racquet Sports. These are merged source sections, not omissions.
- Playbook Wristband is present as Playbook Wristbands.
- Gun Rods, Gun Slings, Gun Cleaning Solutions, Range Bags & Packs, and Projectiles each appear twice in the PDF with different formulas. The JSON stores one version of each. Earlier source versions have attributes; later versions have blank attributes.
- Swiss Balls and Golf Clubs have blank attributes in the source too.
- Yoga Wedges contains the likely attribute typo Assembled ProductWidth.
- The JSON source metadata names SportsOutdoorsContentStyleGuide.pdf rather than the supplied PDF, and status remains partial.
- This audit verifies family and product coverage; it does not certify every formula token or footnote.
